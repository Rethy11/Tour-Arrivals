About

A private, purpose-built tool

What this is

Tour Arrivals is a small display tool built for one specific business, to show front-of-house staff the day's upcoming tour arrivals — arrival time, expected guest count, and assigned crew — in a large, glanceable format.

How it's used

Staff load a saved schedule export at the start of the day, and the tool displays each upcoming arrival in turn, with reminders to confirm when guests actually arrive.

Who this is for

This isn't a general product — it was built for one business's specific workflow and internal terminology, and isn't intended for use outside that context.
